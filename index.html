05 — FAQ
What if I miss a filing deadline?

I track every client's deadlines and follow up with reminders well in advance, so this is rare. If something is genuinely at risk, I'll flag it to you directly rather than let it slip.

Do you work with businesses outside Dharwad?

Yes — most of the work is done remotely over WhatsApp, email, or call, so location isn't usually a barrier.

I don't have my books organized — can you still help?

Yes, that's common. Send what you have, even if it's messy, and I'll help sort it out from there.

How do I get a quote for my specific case?

Reach out with a brief description of what you need — GST filing, an audit, books to maintain — and I'll give you a clear answer and pricing directly.
